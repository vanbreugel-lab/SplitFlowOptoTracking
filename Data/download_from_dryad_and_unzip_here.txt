================================================================================
 NOTES
================================================================================

- Full variable descriptions for every file are in README_dryad.md (available
  at the Dryad link above).

- The 3D CFD velocity field archives (3D_vel_1.zip – 3D_vel_6.zip) are large
  (~4 GB each) and are only needed if you intend to re-run the trajectory
  simulations. Figure generation notebooks do not require them directly.

- All paths in the analysis notebooks are set relative to the repo root and
  assume data files are placed exactly as shown above.
